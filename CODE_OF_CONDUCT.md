# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and keep discussion on the work. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Two project-specific expectations:
- **Don't share personal information** in issues, PRs, screenshots or test data. Redact it. That includes your own clients' data.
- **Don't shame.** People ask about compliance because they want to do the right thing. Answer the question.

Report concerns privately to the lead maintainer (see GOVERNANCE.md). Reports are handled confidentially.
